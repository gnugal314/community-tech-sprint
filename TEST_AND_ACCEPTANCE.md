# Deliverable Review and Acceptance Plan

The primary outputs are an assessment, recommendations, a future-state workflow, and an implementation plan. Acceptance therefore focuses on quality, traceability, feasibility, responsible-AI controls, usability, and ownership. Apply software testing only if an approved prototype is included.

## Definition of ready for design

- McCall has confirmed the need statement and desired outcomes.
- The current workflow, stakeholders, pain points, root causes, and constraints are sufficiently understood.
- In-scope and out-of-scope boundaries are documented.
- Success criteria and final deliverable formats are agreed.
- Required materials and decision-makers are available.

## Definition of done for each deliverable

- Content addresses the approved need and scope.
- Important claims are supported by McCall input, source materials, or clearly labeled assumptions.
- Recommendations are specific, prioritized, and feasible.
- Dependencies, limitations, risks, owners, and next steps are visible.
- Privacy, security, compliance, governance, human oversight, and change implications are addressed where relevant.
- Technical language is explained in plain language.
- McCall feedback has been incorporated or dispositioned.
- The artifact is editable, legible, internally consistent, and stored in the approved location.

## Integrated review checklist

| Review area | Acceptance question |
|---|---|
| Problem fit | Does the package solve the validated problem rather than an assumed one? |
| Current-state accuracy | Does McCall recognize the workflow, burden, controls, and root causes? |
| Recommendation traceability | Can every priority recommendation be tied to a pain point or outcome? |
| Feasibility | Are systems, data, effort, cost categories, skills, approvals, and dependencies considered? |
| Responsible AI | Are human review, limitations, escalation, monitoring, explainability, and accountability defined? |
| Privacy/security/compliance | Are sensitive-data boundaries and required control reviews identified? |
| Organizational readiness | Are ownership, training, change, governance, and support needs realistic? |
| Implementation readiness | Does the roadmap specify sequence, prerequisites, owners, measures, and decision gates? |
| Usability | Can intended McCall stakeholders understand and use the deliverables? |
| Sustainability | Can McCall own the next steps without relying on the volunteer team? |

## Recommendation quality scale

- **Accept:** Accurate, relevant, feasible, and ready for inclusion.
- **Accept with revision:** Direction is sound; a specific correction is required before handoff.
- **Defer:** Potentially valuable but insufficiently validated or outside current capacity.
- **Reject:** Misaligned, unsafe, infeasible, duplicative, or outside scope.

## Conditional prototype testing

If a prototype is approved, document tests for representative sanitized inputs, expected outputs, invalid or missing data, access controls, human review, error handling, auditability, and rollback. A prototype must be labeled as a demonstration and must not be connected to live sensitive data or operational systems without separate authorization.
