# Communication and Collaboration Plan

## Objectives

- Build trust between volunteers and McCall stakeholders.
- Maintain shared understanding of scope, decisions, risks, owners, and next steps.
- Protect sensitive information through approved channels.
- Enable rapid feedback without creating unnecessary meetings.

## Communication cadence

| Audience | Purpose | Timing | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volunteer team | Introductions, strengths, norms, preliminary roles, and discovery preparation | 30-minute call by September 11 | Team lead |
| Full project team | Relationship-building, McCall overview, challenge discussion, draft need statement, and prework | 30–45-minute call by September 11 | Team lead |
| Volunteer team | Final preparation, materials review, and question consolidation | By September 15; asynchronous unless a call is needed | Team lead/workstream owners |

| Program team | Status, blockers, and support requests | When requested or immediately for material risk | Team lead |
| Participants | Debrief and survey | September 17 and by September 25 | All participants |

## Full-team connector call format

- 10 minutes: welcome, purpose, agenda, introductions, and brief McCall overview
- 20 minutes: challenge discussion, draft need statement, potential strategies, and preliminary scope
- 5 minutes: open Q&A
- 5 minutes: assignments, required materials, and next steps

## Working-session check-ins

Use short alignment points at phase transitions:

1. What has been verified?
2. What remains uncertain?
3. What decision is required now?
4. Are scope, time, and risk still acceptable?
5. Who owns the next output and when is it due?

## Shared-material practices

- Use the approved shared folder for background documents and working materials.
- Use GitHub only for approved, non-sensitive project artifacts.
- Do not email or commit identifiable incident information, credentials, confidential narratives, or unapproved internal documents.
- Use clear filenames and one designated source of truth for each deliverable.
- Workstream owners update their assigned artifact; the team lead or delegate performs final integration.

## Escalation triggers

Notify the team lead immediately when:

- A blocker may consume more than 15 minutes or threaten a phase outcome.
- A necessary McCall decision-maker, document, or system contact is unavailable.
- The requested work exceeds the approved scope or event capacity.
- Sensitive information, unsafe AI use, or a privacy/security/compliance concern appears.
- A recommendation cannot be implemented or sustained within known constraints.
- Team conflict is affecting progress or inclusion.

The team lead will determine whether to resolve, work around, reduce scope, document the limitation, or contact the program planning team.

## Collaboration norms

- Listen to understand and invite input from every participant.
- Use plain language and explain technical terms.
- Challenge ideas respectfully; do not personalize disagreement.
- Confirm McCall’s understanding and agreement at each decision point.
- Be candid about feasibility, risk, uncertainty, and limitations.
- End each session with documented owners and next steps.
