# Handoff and Closeout Checklist

## Final package

- [ ] Validated need statement and approved scope included
- [ ] Current-state workflow and root causes included
- [ ] Prioritized opportunity matrix included
- [ ] Future-state workflow identifies human review, exceptions, escalation, and controls
- [ ] Privacy, security, compliance, governance, and change considerations included
- [ ] Implementation roadmap includes prerequisites, sequencing, owners, decision gates, and measures
- [ ] Assumptions, limitations, deferred items, and unresolved questions clearly labeled
- [ ] Deliverables use plain language and are consistent with each other
- [ ] McCall feedback incorporated or dispositioned

## Ownership and access

- [ ] McCall business owner named
- [ ] Technical/data/security contact named
- [ ] Implementation/change owner named
- [ ] Final editable files stored in an approved location accessible to McCall
- [ ] Any links and permissions tested
- [ ] Temporary volunteer access removed or expiration documented when appropriate
- [ ] No sensitive data, confidential narratives, or credentials included in GitHub

## Implementation readiness

- [ ] Recommended first step and decision owner identified
- [ ] Required policy, privacy, security, compliance, architecture, or procurement reviews identified
- [ ] Data standardization and quality prerequisites documented
- [ ] Pilot boundaries and representative test data approach documented
- [ ] Human-review responsibilities and escalation path documented
- [ ] Training, communications, and change impacts documented
- [ ] Cost categories, staffing needs, dependencies, and support model identified at an appropriate level
- [ ] Success measures and monitoring approach defined
- [ ] Expansion beyond the Quality Department requires separate evaluation and approval

## Walkthrough and knowledge transfer

- [ ] Final deliverables presented to relevant McCall stakeholders
- [ ] Recommendations and prioritization rationale explained
- [ ] Future-state workflow reviewed step by step
- [ ] Implementation roadmap and decision gates reviewed
- [ ] McCall questions answered or assigned for follow-up
- [ ] Relevant implementation stakeholders understand their roles
- [ ] McCall confirms ownership and ability to use the package

## Closeout

- [ ] Formal acceptance or follow-up conditions recorded
- [ ] Decision log updated
- [ ] RAID items closed, transferred, or assigned
- [ ] Deferred work preserved in the backlog
- [ ] Volunteer team and McCall complete the September 17 debrief
- [ ] Volunteers log service time by September 25
- [ ] Participants complete the post-program survey by September 25
- [ ] Contributions and partnership recognized

## Recommended post-event checkpoints

These checkpoints are recommendations for McCall’s implementation owner, not commitments by the volunteer team.

| Timing | Suggested decision |
|---|---|
| 30 days | Confirm owner, governance path, data readiness, and selected first pilot |
| 60–90 days | Review pilot design, controls, user involvement, and success measures before launch |
| After pilot | Evaluate quality, time saved, user experience, control performance, and whether to continue, revise, expand, or retire |
