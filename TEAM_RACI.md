## Decision method

1. Seek consensus using verified evidence, McCall context, and scoring criteria.
2. McCall decides business priorities, acceptable tradeoffs, and final acceptance.
3. The team lead decides facilitation, sequencing, and work allocation.
4. Technical or control concerns must be resolved or explicitly documented before recommendation.
5. When time is constrained, the team lead proposes a path and confirms McCall agreement.

## Team agreements to confirm

- Preferred communication channel and response expectations
- Meeting attendance and camera/participation norms
- How disagreement and conflict will be handled
- Secure shared-folder location and access
- File naming, version control, and review ownership
- How blockers and risks will be escalated
- How the team will coordinate work across the two half-days

## Blocker rule

Raise blockers immediately. After 15 minutes without progress, notify the team lead. The lead will remove the blocker, identify a workaround, reduce scope, or escalate to the program or McCall decision owner.
