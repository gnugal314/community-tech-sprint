# Rapid Impact Challenge Schedule

All official event times are Eastern Time. The internal phase allocations below are recommended working blocks and may be adjusted with McCall while preserving the official Zoom schedule.

## Pre-event milestones

| Due | Activity | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|
| September 1 | Training webinar | Understand program structure, skills-based volunteering expectations, and toolkit |
| By September 11 | 30-minute volunteer connector call | Introductions, strengths, working norms, preliminary roles, and discovery preparation |
| By September 11 | 30–45-minute full-team connector call | Relationship-building, organization overview, initial challenge discussion, and next steps |
| By September 15 | Background and access review | Relevant documents reviewed, approved access confirmed, and discovery questions consolidated |

| Time | Activity | Output |
|---:|---|---|

| 0:20–0:25 | Collaboration norms and decision method | Team agreements |
| 0:25–0:30 | McCall project discussion and discovery preparation | Initial questions and assignments |

## Full-team connector call — 40 minutes

| Time | Activity | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Welcome, purpose, agenda, introductions, and brief McCall overview | Shared context and rapport |

| 0:30–0:35 | Open Q&A | Unanswered questions and required materials |
| 0:35–0:40 | Close and next steps | Owners and pre-Day 1 actions |
